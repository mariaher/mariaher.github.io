### Independent
At the University of Münster, research assistants are usually not permitted to teach courses and grade students on their own. However, the faculty council can decide to make an exception. During my time as a PhD students, two such exceptions were made for me. In both cases, I substituted for lecturers who were on research leave.

<div class="cv-entry">
  <div class="cv-date">Summer 2025</div>
  <div class="cv-content">
    <strong>Introduction to C/C++</strong><br>
    The course provides an introduction on how to program in C and mainly in C++ for about 100 students. At the end of the semester, the students are able to write a small game in C++ using the <a href="https://www.sfml-dev.org/" target="_blank">SFML library</a>.
  </div>
</div>
<div class="cv-entry">
  <div class="cv-date">Summer 2023</div>
  <div class="cv-content">
    <strong id="coaching">Coaching for Teaching Assistants in Computer Science</strong><br>
    This course aids new student teaching assistants in preparing and teaching their first computer science tutorial. After a short block seminar at the beginning of the semester, the students are supervised in two of their tutorials during the semester to get feedback on their teaching methods. 
    In that semester, I taught the block seminar.
  </div>
</div>

### As a Teaching Assistant
Unless stated otherwise, all of these courses were organized by the <i>Algorithms and Education</i> group by Prof. Jan Vahrenhold.
<div class="cv-entry">
  <div class="cv-date">Summer 2026</div>
  <div class="cv-content">
    <strong>Seminar on Topological Data Analysis</strong><br>
    In this block seminar, we covered topics like persistent homology, reeb graphs and mapper graphs. I researched the topics, and supervised and guided the students. For this seminar, we cooperated with Prof. Michael Joachim from the Mathematical Institute of the University of Münster.
  </div>
</div>
<div class="cv-entry">
  <div class="cv-date">Winter 2025/26</div>
  <div class="cv-content">
    <strong>Resource-efficient Algorithms</strong><br>
    This course covers the design and implementation of memory-efficient algorithms. Students learn about machine models that incorporate ressource management and learned how to employ C++-techniques to write efficient and clean code.
  </div>
</div>
<div class="cv-entry">
  <div class="cv-date">Summer 2025</div>
  <div class="cv-content">
    <strong id="LeCe">LearningCenter Computer Science</strong><br>
    Since 2019, the <a href="https://www.uni-muenster.de/Informatik/en/learningcenter/index.shtml" target="_blank">LearningCenter</a> supports students during their first few semesters in Computer Science. Students can ask questions and get additional material for their studies. My task was coordinating with the student assistants and organizing a weekly course that reviewed the contents of "Algorithms and Data Structures".
  </div>
</div>
<div class="cv-entry">
  <div class="cv-date">Winter 2024/25</div>
  <div class="cv-content">
    <strong>Capstone Project: "Coach in a Pocket – Developing an Anti-Procrastination Tool"</strong><br>
    For this project we cooperated with the <a href="https://www.uni-muenster.de/Prokrastinationsambulanz/en/index.html" target="_blank">Prokrastinationsambulanz</a> of the University of Münster to develop an app that helps students track and battle their procrastination habits. The app was developed by a group of students in Dart/Flutter and contained surveys, information texts, and exercises. I supervised the group and provided technical assistance, whenever necessary.
